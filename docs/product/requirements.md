# KenFit Product Requirements

## Product

KenFit

## Product Type

Mobile-first fitness platform.

## Target User

People who are beginning or improving their fitness journey
and need guidance to structure their training and nutrition.

## Core Problem

Many people entering fitness do not know:

- Which training split to use
- Which exercises to perform
- How many sets and repetitions to use
- How often to train
- How to structure their nutrition
- How to track their progress

## Solution

KenFit collects information about the user and generates a
personalized fitness plan based on:

- Fitness goal
- Experience level
- Availability
- Equipment
- Physical characteristics
- Preferences

## MVP

The first version will include:

- User registration
- Authentication
- Fitness onboarding
- Goal selection
- Personalized split
- Workout plan
- Workout tracking
- Progress tracking
- Basic nutrition guidance

## Future Features

- AI assistant
- Adaptive workout plans
- Advanced nutrition
- Food tracking
- Notifications
- Gamification
- Premium subscription
- Advanced analytics
